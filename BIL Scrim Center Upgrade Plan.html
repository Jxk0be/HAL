Upgrading BIL with Scrim Center's look

BIL is already deeper than Scrim Center where it counts for a league: double-entry reporting, recusal, the audit log, preseason, 8s and a full Discord bot. What Scrim Center has is the feel: an icon-rail app shell, a live results ticker, giant display titles, striped cards, a season hub, a podium leaderboard and a personal dashboard.

So this plan is mostly a reskin, plus 4 new screens. It keeps the Blackhat red and changes no league rules.

Every prompt starts with “Read CLAUDE.md first” and ends by updating CLAUDE.md and the app overview, like your existing guide. Phase 0 switches headings to Chakra Petch and body text to Barlow, which is most of Scrim Center's feel. To keep Barlow Condensed and Inter, delete that part of the prompt.

Decisions baked in

One red, not five colors

Scrim Center tints every feature card a different color. CLAUDE.md allows one red accent, with green and coral only for results. The plan keeps that: cards get a red or neutral top border. Scrim Center gets its energy from type scale and stripes as much as from color.

Rail on desktop, tab bar on phones

Your header lists 10 links. The rail holds 7 primary items plus “More”. Home and Login use a slim public top bar like Scrim Center's; every other page uses the rail. Phones get a bottom tab bar, since players check schedules there.

No XP system

Scrim Center ranks players by XP and its own tiers. BIL has real stats and the player's actual CoD rank, which is more meaningful. The podium uses your leaderboard's existing sort, and the RankBadge takes the place of their tier emblems.

Season hub collects pages

Scrim Center puts a season on one page with tabs. The new /season hub reuses your Standings, Matches, Playoffs and Rules components inside tabs. The old routes stay so existing links and bot posts don't break.

Upgrade phases

Run them in order; each one ships on its own. Check phases off as you finish them (progress saves in this browser). Rough estimate with Claude Code: phases 0–3 take a weekend and change the whole feel. Phases 4–10 take about another weekend.

Not porting, and why

Switches, coins, store, payouts

Real money and coin systems bring payment and legal work. BIL pays one $250 prize by PayPal or Venmo, which is fine by hand.

Talent Hub

Your casting desk already covers casters. A booking marketplace only makes sense if BIL gets outside talent.

Multi-game, ELO, FACEIT tabs

BIL is one game with one ruleset. Those tabs would sit empty.

Gotchas

New columns need grants

seasons.banner_url and teams.banner_url follow your own rule: column grants, the audit trigger, regenerated types and a check in audit_strip(). A missing grant breaks the whole team load.

Ticker stays cheap

Use one shared realtime subscription for the ticker, the way useLiveBroadcasts() already works. Don't open a channel per component, and leave preseason out of the ticker like every other aggregate.

Fonts and the CSP

Install the new fonts through Fontsource like the current ones, so netlify.toml's CSP doesn't change. If you link Google Fonts instead, both hosts have to go into the CSP.

Bot links

Bot embeds link to /matches/:id, /standings and /my-team. Keep all of those routes working; the hub only adds new ones.
