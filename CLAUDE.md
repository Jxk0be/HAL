# Blackhat Invitational League (BIL): project spec and conventions

Website and serverless Discord bot for Blackhat Invitational League (BIL), a competitive Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4 league.
This file is the only context future sessions get. Keep it complete and update it when a decision changes.
The step-by-step build plan is `hal-build-guide.html` (kept outside the repo); each step's prompt starts with "Read CLAUDE.md first".

## Repo layout (pnpm workspaces)
- `apps/web`: Vue 3 + TypeScript (strict, `noUncheckedIndexedAccess`) + Vite + Tailwind CSS v4 (`@tailwindcss/vite`, tokens in `@theme` in `src/assets/main.css`) + Vue Router + Pinia + `@supabase/supabase-js`.
  - `src/config/site.ts`: site config and league copy (name, Discord invite, rulebook repo). Don't hard-code copy in components.
  - `src/lib/supabase.ts`: the single Supabase client. `src/composables/`: data access. `src/stores/`: Pinia (session/user only).

- `rules/rulebook.md`: the league rulebook, the source of truth for rule text, rendered by the site's Rules page.
- `scripts/`: one-off Node/TS scripts (for example, Discord slash command registration). Run with `tsx`.
- `.env.example` (root: scripts, CLI, Discord secrets) and `apps/web/.env.example` (browser-safe `VITE_*` values). No real secrets in git.

## League
- Name: Blackhat Invitational League (BIL; "Blackhat" in casual copy). Rebranded from Heaven's Arena League (HAL) in Nov 2026: internal identifiers (`hal.*` settings flags, `halEmbed`, `HAL_WIN`/`HAL_LOSS`, migration names, the `hal-web` launch config) keep the old prefix on purpose. Look: black and gray with one red accent; hooded-figure emblem.
- Game: Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4. CDL ruleset and Gentlemen's Agreements (GAs). Modes, maps and GAs are admin-editable data (the owner supplies them later). Never hard-code them.
- Region: NA only. League times are defined in America/New_York and shown in each viewer's local time.
- Entry: free. Prize: $250 to the champion ($62.50 per starter), paid by PayPal or Venmo within 7 days of the final.

## Season format
- Up to 16 teams in one division. If more than 16 sign up, split into Premier and Contender. The data model must support multiple divisions per season.
- Preseason (2 weeks), then an 8-week regular season with 2 matches per week, then playoffs (top 8, 2 weeks), then offseason.
- Default match nights: Tuesday and Thursday at 9:00 PM America/New_York (8 PM Central). Store these as season settings, not constants.
- Series are best-of-5. Higher seed picks first; picks alternate.
- Reschedules: both captains must agree through the site or bot at least 24 hours before the default time. The new time must be in the same week, by Sunday 11:59 PM ET. No agreement means the default time stands.

## Eligibility, rosters, subs
- 18+ (age confirmed at signup). Controller only on every platform. PC is allowed with a controller.
- Admins may ask a PC player for a live screen-share. (There is no POV-recording pledge; it was removed.)
- Login is Discord OAuth only. Players add their Activision ID to their profile.
- One team per player per season. Roster: up to 4 starters (the captain is one of them) and 3 subs, so max 7. One captain, one optional co-captain.
- Joining a team: everyone who signs in with Discord is automatically a free agent. A captain invites a free agent, either by searching their name on the team page or with Invite on the Free Agents page, and only while the team has an open spot. The player accepts or declines (on the site, later also in Discord). Accepting puts them on the roster immediately with no admin approval, takes them off the free agent list and cancels their other invites. Invites expire after 7 days.
- Leaving a team: captains remove players, and players can leave on their own (the captain transfers the captaincy first). Either way the player goes back on the free agent list.
- 3 roster changes (adds or drops) per team per season. Rosters lock about 2 weeks before playoffs (season setting). Playoff eligibility requires 2+ regular-season matches played.
- Subs must be on the roster 24 hours before a match. Swaps only between maps.
- Emergency sub: if a team has fewer than 4 eligible players, an admin can approve one free agent for one match only.
- A dropped player can't join another team in the same division for 7 days.

## Match day
- Bot reminders at 24h and 1h before each match, in the team's private channel.
- Check-in opens 15 minutes before the match. The captain selects exactly 4 players from the roster plus approved subs. Admins can audit check-ins.
- 15 minutes late = map forfeit; 30 minutes late = series forfeit.
- Disconnect in the first 30 seconds or before the first kill = map restart. Otherwise play continues; one tactical pause of up to 5 minutes per team per series.
- 2 forfeits in a season = team removed and its remaining matches recorded as losses.

## Reporting and stats (double entry)
- Recorded: series map count and winner; per map: mode, map, final score, winner, screenshot; per player per map: kills, deaths, score.
- The site calculates K/D, +/-, average score per map, maps played, map and series win %, and team records by mode.
- Both captains submit the full scoreboard (all 8 players, every map) with screenshots.
- A series result is confirmed when both captains' series and map scores agree; standings update then.
- Stats are compared field by field. Matching values are confirmed. Differing values create mismatch rows for the admin queue, where an admin picks the value after checking the screenshots.
- If one captain hasn't submitted 24 hours after the match, the other captain's submission stands, as long as it has screenshots.

## Integrity
- Cheat devices (Cronus, XIM, strike packs): team banned for the season, player banned permanently. Conduct violations: suspension or ban. Exploits: map loss. Admins may request VODs.
- Disputes are opened by a captain within 24 hours, with evidence. Admin decisions are final.
- The owner's team plays in HAL. Staff can NEVER take staff actions on a match involving a team they are rostered on. Enforce this in Postgres (RLS and functions), not just the UI.
- Public audit log: every staff write records actor, action, table, row, old value, new value and time. Anyone can view it. Private fields (emails, Discord IDs, dispute evidence) are never exposed.

## Roles
- Owner: everything (seasons, settings, roles, payouts, all admin actions), except actions on their own team's matches.
- Admin: mismatches, disputes, roster approvals, add/edit/remove matches, results, teams and free agent posts, penalties. Not roles or seasons.
- Caster: mark a match as broadcast and paste their stream link (shows a live banner). Nothing else.
- Captain: manage own team, check in, report, reschedule, open disputes.
- Player: profile, free agent post, join team.

## Discord
- Separate league server (rebuilt for the Blackhat rebrand, Nov 2026). Channels (full list and text in `docs/discord-server-text.md`):
  - Info: #start-here, #rules, #announcements
  - League (bot boards): #team-rosters, #standings, #match-schedule, #results, #player-of-the-week
  - Community: #general, #free-agents (/lfg), #teams-recruiting, #sub-board, #clips, #suggestions
  - Matches: one private text and voice channel per team, created and removed by the bot
  - Support: #disputes (bot-created private threads), staff: #admin-only, #audit-log
  - Voice: Lobby, Waiting Room, Casting Stage, Admin
- Live boards (`_shared/boards.ts` builders, `_shared/board_sync.ts` I/O), run at the end of every discord-scheduler pass: one message per approved team (#team-rosters), per division (#standings), per season week (#match-schedule, kept as history), per short team per match (#sub-board), and a Monday-noon #player-of-the-week post (`week_top_players`, 2+ maps, best average score). Message ids and a content hash live in `discord_boards` (service role only); `reconcile()` posts new boards, edits only when the hash changes (timestamps ignored), reposts if a message was deleted by hand, and deletes boards no longer wanted unless `keep`. Channel ids are `app_settings.discord_channels` keys `rosters`, `standings`, `schedule`, `subs`, `potw` (Admin → Discord); a family with no channel is skipped.
- #sub-board: `sub_board()` lists teams with players marked out of a match in the next 7 days that still need subs (min(4 − available, 2) minus emergency-sub requests not declined/denied). "I'm available" / "Withdraw" (`subs:yes|no:<match>:<team>`) call `volunteer_for_sub_as` (`can_emergency_sub`: not on a team this season, not banned, profile complete), redraw the post and tell the team channel (captain pings). Volunteers live in `sub_volunteers` (service role only); captains still request the player on the match page.
- Map pool: `game_modes`, `maps`, `map_modes` (which maps each mode plays; public read, admins write). Edited mode-first on Admin → Modes & maps (`/admin/maps`, `GameDataPanel`), shown publicly on `/maps`. League reports and 8s both use it.
- 8s (casual 4v4 pick-ups, #lf-8s): tables `eights_queue`, `eights_matches`, `eights_players`, `eights_reports` (RLS on, service role only) and service-role functions `eights_join` (advisory-locked; any linked account with an Activision ID, staff-only included, not banned, not in a live 8s match; spots expire after 2h; at 8 it shuffles 4v4 and opens a 2-minute mode vote: status `voting`, `vote_ends_at`), `eights_vote(user, match, mode)` (players in the match; modes from `eights_vote_modes()` = active modes with an active map; the 8th vote closes it), `eights_close_vote` (most votes, ties/no votes random, then a random active map from that mode's `map_modes`; status `live`), `eights_tick()` (pg_cron job `bil-eights-tick` every 15 seconds: closes expired votes and POSTs `eights_votes_closed` to discord-sync, which redraws the posts), `eights_request_cancel(user, match)` (players in a `voting`/`live` match toggle a request in `eights_cancel_requests`; 5 of 8 cancels it with `resolved_by` null, as if it never happened), `eights_leave`, `eights_report` (only once `live`) (2 agreeing reports per team confirm, any disagreement → `disputed`), `eights_resolve(actor, match, winner|null)` (`is_admin_for`, null cancels, writes the audit log) and `eights_leaderboard` (wins, then win %). Bot: builders in `_shared/eights.ts` (buttons `eights:join|leave|board` (join doubles as "Queue again" on settled matches), `eights:vote:<match>:<mode>`, `eights:cancel:<match>`, `eights:won:<match>:<a|b>`, `eights:fix:<match>:<a|b|cancel>`), I/O in `_shared/eights_flow.ts`, handlers in `discord-interactions/eights.ts`, command `/8s join|leave|leaderboard`. The queue post is board family `eights` (redrawn on every join/leave too). On start the bot posts the match (pinging the 8) and moves players already in voice into the `eights_team_a` / `eights_team_b` channels (needs Move Members; error 40032 = not in voice, ignored). Channel keys `eights`, `eights_team_a`, `eights_team_b`.
- Preseason (optional, cast matches before the regular season): a scheduled preseason match is a real `matches` row with stage `preseason` and week 0 (`matches_week_check`), so reminders, check-in, full double-entry reporting, disputes and the Casting desk work unchanged. Preseason is excluded everywhere results count: `division_standings` (regular only), `player_season_stats_v`, `team_maps_played_v`, `team_mode_records_v`, `season_leaderboard`, `week_top_players`, team forfeit counts (`admin_forfeit` / `admin_clear_result`) and the #match-schedule board; keep it out of any new aggregate. Labels use `weekLabel()` (web `lib/preseason.ts`, bot `match_messages.ts`): week 0 reads "Preseason". Flow: (1) Admin → Preseason (`/admin/preseason`) calls `preseason_send_invites(season)` (signups only): a `preseason_interest` row per approved team + an invite in its team channel with `preseason:in|out:<team>` buttons; teams approved later get one via the `teams_preseason_invite` trigger. Captains/co-captains answer with `preseason_set_interest` (site, `PreseasonCard` on My team) or the buttons (`preseason_set_interest_as`). (2) `preseason_generate(season, replace)` puts the yes-teams in a random circle (2 matches each, 1 for two teams) in `preseason_pairings` (no times), sets `seasons.preseason_generated_at` (answers close) and the bot creates a private shared channel per pair in the "Preseason" category (`app_settings.discord_preseason`; both team roles, BIL Admin, BIL Caster). (3) A captain proposes with `preseason_propose` (site) or `/preseason match day time` (`preseason_propose_as`) into `preseason_proposals` (one pending per pairing); the other team's captain answers (`preseason_respond[_as]`, buttons `preseason:accept|decline:<proposal>`); accepting creates or moves the match. `preseason_conflict()` refuses any time within 2 hours of another non-cancelled match in the season (one cast at a time); proposals must be ≥1h out and before `seasons.starts_on`. Reschedule requests are blocked for preseason matches. `preseason_cancel_pairing` (admin) cancels. Outbox events `preseason_invite`, `preseason_pairing`, `preseason_proposal` (`event` proposed|answered), `preseason_pairing_cancelled`, `preseason_channel_removed` are handled in `_shared/preseason_flow.ts` (builders in `preseason_messages.ts`).
- The bot is serverless: Discord HTTP interactions (slash commands, buttons, select menus, modals) are served by a Supabase Edge Function, the Discord REST API handles outbound actions, and pg_cron runs scheduled jobs. No gateway process.
- Launch features: reminders, check-in, results announcements, role sync (team, captain, free agent and staff roles plus team channels).
- In-season features: `/lfg`, `/reschedule`, `/report` (series and map scores plus screenshots in Discord, then a button opens the site's prefilled stat grid), `/dispute` (private thread with both captains and admins).
- Discord modals hold at most 5 inputs, so the 8-player stat grid lives on the site.

## Conventions
- Vue: Composition API with `<script setup lang="ts">` only. Type props/emits with `defineProps<...>()` / `defineEmits<...>()`. Small composables per domain (`useTeams`, `useMatches`, and so on) that wrap Supabase queries and return typed refs plus `loading`/`error`. Components never import `supabase` directly. Pinia only for session and user state.
- No `any` unless justified in a comment.
- Styling: Tailwind only, using tokens from `@theme` (`bg-bg`, `bg-surface`, `text-muted`, `text-accent`, `bg-brand text-on-brand`, ...). No component libraries; build shared primitives in `src/components/ui`.
- Supabase types are generated to `apps/web/src/types/database.ts` (`pnpm db:types` or the MCP `generate_typescript_types`). Regenerate after every migration.
- Times: `timestamptz` in UTC in the database; league scheduling math in America/New_York; display in the viewer's local time with the ET time on hover.
- Every permission is enforced in Postgres RLS or SECURITY DEFINER functions. UI checks are only for convenience. RLS is enabled on every table.
- SECURITY DEFINER functions always `set search_path = ''` and fully qualify names.
- Every staff write goes through the audit log.
- Migrations are additive and named with timestamps (`supabase/migrations/<version>_<name>.sql`). Never edit an applied migration. Run the Supabase security advisors after DDL changes.
- Edge Functions are Deno TypeScript. Shared code lives in `supabase/functions/_shared`.
- Accessible, responsive UI: labels on inputs, visible focus states, keyboard-friendly modals. Mobile matters (players check schedules on phones); every page must work at 375px wide.
- `localStorage`/`sessionStorage` access is always wrapped in try/catch.

  - `_shared/dispute_flow.ts`: `ensureDisputeThread` creates one private thread per dispute in #disputes (`app_settings.discord_channels.disputes`), claimed in `bot_events` (`dispute:<id>`) and stored in `disputes.discord_thread_id`. Members come from `dispute_thread_people(dispute)`: both teams' captains and co-captains, plus owners/admins not on either team; recused admins are counted and a "Recused staff excluded" note is posted. Admins are added and pinged individually (not the BIL Admin role, which would pull recused admins in). The opening post has a "Resolve (admins)" link to /admin/disputes (decisions are written on the site so they're audited).
  - Scheduler: `dispute_opened` (site disputes) → thread; `dispute_comment` → mirrors site comments into the thread; `dispute_resolved` → posts the decision, archives and locks; `dispute_reopened` → unlocks. Discord thread messages are not copied to the site (no gateway).
  - The bot needs Create Private Threads, Send Messages in Threads and Manage Threads in #disputes, and captains need to see #disputes.

## Database (as built)
- Permission helpers (SECURITY DEFINER, signed-in only; anon can't execute them): `is_owner()`, `is_admin()` (owner or admin), `is_caster()`, `has_role(role)`, `my_team_ids(season)`, `is_captain_of(team)` (captain or co-captain), `is_on_team(team)`, `is_captain_in_match(match, team)`, `match_captain_team(match)`, `is_party_to_match(match)`.
- **Recusal:** every staff write on a match or its children uses `can_staff_match(match_id)` (= `is_admin() and not is_party_to_match`); staff writes on teams/rosters use `can_staff_team(team_id)`. This applies to the owner too.
- Policies on anon-readable tables must not call the helpers (anon has no EXECUTE): give anon its own `using (true)` / plain-column policy.
- Rule triggers (`*_guard`) raise clear errors (errcode P0001 for rule breaks, 42501 for permission). They let the service role through (`auth.uid()` null) and our own functions set the transaction flag `hal.internal` for internal writes (`public.is_internal()`).
- Rosters: the team creator becomes captain and first starter automatically. Nobody inserts `team_members` directly except staff; players join through `team_invites` using the RPCs `invite_player(team, user, role?)`, `respond_to_invite(invite, accept)` and `cancel_invite(invite)`. Players leave through `remove_player(team, user)` (captains/staff) or `leave_team(team)`. `team_open_spots(team)` returns `{open, starters_open, subs_open}` for showing or hiding Invite buttons. Role caps are season settings (`max_starters` 4, `max_subs` 3, `max_roster` 7). An invite with no role becomes a starter if a starter spot is open, otherwise a sub. Accepting needs a complete profile (Activision ID plus age confirmation). Captains can update a member's starter/sub role directly, within the caps. `roster_requests` is now only for emergency subs (admin-approved). Roster changes count only while the season is `regular` or `playoffs`.
- Free agents: `profiles.is_free_agent` defaults to true and a trigger keeps it in sync with roster membership. The public list is the `free_agents` view (profile plus optional `free_agent_posts` details, banned players excluded). Captains search it by name (`discord_username`/`activision_id`).
- Disputes: evidence lives in `dispute_evidence` (parties + staff only); the public reads `disputes`/`disputes_public` without it.
- Private columns are hidden with column privileges, so `select('*')` fails on `profiles` (no `discord_id`) and `disputes` (no `discord_thread_id`). Select explicit columns or use `profiles_public` / `disputes_public`. **A new column on these tables is invisible to the API until you grant it** (`grant select (col) on public.profiles to anon, authenticated;`, plus `update (col)` if users write it); a missing grant makes the whole profile load fail.
- Casters change matches only through `set_broadcast(match_id, live, url)` and `set_vod(match_id, url)` (audited, https only).
- Emergency subs (rule 4.3): captain marks players out (`match_absences`) and requests on the match page in one call (`request_emergency_sub`; `EmergencySubCard`). `roster_requests_guard` allows it when `available_players()` (eligible roster minus marked out) is below 4, caps open+approved requests at 2 per team per match, and refuses approval beyond `least(2, 4 - available)`. Marked-out players are excluded from `eligible_checkin_players`/`checkins_guard`, and can't be put back once a sub is approved (`match_absences_guard`). Other checks: player a free agent with a complete profile, not banned, match not over, no duplicate. Then → the free agent accepts/declines (`respond_emergency_sub` on Profile, or `respond_emergency_sub_as` from the DM buttons `esub:accept|decline:<id>`) → an admin approves on Roster approvals (the guard refuses approval until `player_confirmed_at` is set; only the respond functions may touch the player columns, via `hal.esub_player`). Declining closes the request as `denied` with `player_declined_at`. `roster_requests_notify` queues `emergency_sub_requested|accepted|declined|reviewed`; the scheduler (`_shared/esub_flow.ts`, messages in `esub_messages.ts`) DMs the player, posts a heads-up in #admin-only on request and a ready-to-approve post once accepted (pinging the BIL Admin role from `app_settings.discord.roles.admin` when the match is < 2h away), and posts outcomes in the team channel. Tests: `supabase/tests/emergency_sub_test.sql`, `emergency_sub_absences_test.sql`.
- Staff-only accounts: `profiles.is_player = false` (owner switches it in Admin → Roles; not while on a team this season). `is_profile_complete()` returns false for them, so every player gate (team creation/joining, /lfg, invite search, emergency subs, Free Agent role) rejects them; `staff_only_guard` triggers on `teams` and `team_members` block them even for admins; `player_directory` and `free_agent_board` skip them; `is_free_agent` is forced off. On the site `auth.isPlayer` is false and they skip player onboarding.
- Skill bracket (rules 2.8–2.10): the league is for Gold to Crimson. `profiles.cod_rank` (enum `cod_rank`: bronze … crimson, iridescent, top250) is the higher of the player's peak rank in the previous CoD and their current rank, set by the player on their profile (column grants: select `cod_rank`, `rank_exception_at`; update `cod_rank`). `is_profile_complete()` requires Gold–Crimson, or Iridescent with `rank_exception_at` set, so every league gate enforces it; `rank_block_reason(user)` gives the message (team join/create and free agent triggers use it). Iridescent exceptions: admins call `set_rank_exception(user, approve)` (Admin → Roster approvals, `RankExceptionsPanel`; audit-logged); `profiles_guard` clears an exception whenever the rank changes and blocks anyone else setting it; picking Iridescent queues `rank_exception_needed` → #admin-only alert. On the site `auth.isOnboarded` means setup is done (any rank) and `auth.canPlay` mirrors `is_profile_complete` (`rankStatus` in `lib/validation/profile.ts`); ineligible players see `AccountNotice`. 8s ignore rank.
- Admin alerts: anything waiting on an admin posts in #admin-only and pings the BIL Admin role (`withAdminPing` + builders in `_shared/admin_alerts.ts`; role id from `app_settings.discord.roles.admin` via `loadAdminRoleId`). Covered: pending team reaching 4 players (`team_pending` from `team_members_ready_notify`; `teams_approval_guard` refuses approval below 4, rule 3.5), report mismatches (`mismatch_opened`, statement trigger on stat_mismatches, one post per match via bot_events `mismatch_admin`), disputes (`dispute_admin:<id>`, alongside the private thread), missing check-in at start (`checkin_missing_admin`), emergency sub ready to approve (`esub_admin`), and matches with no result 24h after the start (scheduler scan, `unreported_admin`). The emergency sub "requested" heads-up is not pinged (nothing to do yet). New admin-facing events must use `withAdminPing`. Tests: `supabase/tests/admin_alerts_test.sql`, `_shared/admin_alerts_test.ts`.
- Reschedule posts @mention the whole roster in each team's own channel (request and outcome) via `withMentions` + `loadRosterDiscordIds`; user mentions only, never roles.
- Season announcements (Admin → Announcements, admins): texts are built from the season settings in `_shared/announcements.ts` (keys `signups_open`, `season_countdown`, `roster_changes`, `roster_lock`, `playoffs`; dates via `eventTime`, playoffs = Monday of week 1 + `regular_weeks`). The page asks discord-sync for `announcements_preview` and posts with `announcement_post` (admin JWT; optional @everyone), so the preview and the post use the same builder. `announcement_settings.auto` toggles automatic posts; the scheduler's `runAutoAnnouncements` posts at `AUTO_OFFSETS` (7 and 1 days before, or on the day) within a 12-hour window via `dueOffsets`, claiming `announcement_posts (season, key, 'auto:<n>d')` so each posts once. Add a new announcement in `announcements.ts` (+ the key check constraint) and its test.
- Finding players to invite (My Team → Invite players, and the emergency sub picker) uses `invitable_players(search)`: anyone signed in with a complete profile, not banned and not on a team in the current season, whether or not `is_free_agent` is ticked. `is_free_agent` ("List me as a free agent") only controls the public Free Agents page, `free_agent_board` and the Free Agent Discord role.
- Team invites DM the player with Accept / Decline (`invite:accept|decline:<id>`), from the site or the #free-agents button: `team_invites_notify` queues `team_invite_sent` / `team_invite_closed`; `_shared/invite_flow.ts` sends the DM (ids kept in bot_events kind `invite:<id>`), edits it when the invite closes, and tells the team channel on accept/decline. The buttons call `respond_to_invite_as`, which runs `respond_to_invite` with the player's claims so the roster guards behave exactly as on the site.
- Help and channel guides: all wording lives in `supabase/functions/_shared/help.ts` (pure; `help_test.ts` checks every command it names is registered in `commands.ts` and that embeds fit Discord's limits). `/help` (public, ephemeral) works out the channel kind from `app_settings.discord_channels` or `teams.discord_channel_id` and adds a note for unlinked users and captains. Admin → Discord → **Post channel guides** calls discord-sync `post_guides` (owner only), which posts or edits one pinned guide per channel (#lfg, #results, #disputes, staff, every approved team channel); message ids are in `app_settings.discord_guides` (`{ channelId: messageId }`). New team channels get a guide when created. After changing commands or guide text: deploy, `pnpm commands:register`, then press the button. Paste-in text for #start-here and #rules is in `docs/discord-server-text.md`; keep its #rules summary in step with the rulebook.
- Audit log: the `audit_staff_change()` trigger is on every league table; add it to any new staff-editable table. It logs writes by staff (`actor_role` = their highest role) and by the service role (`actor_role` 'system', shown as "HAL bot"). Captain and player actions are not logged. Updates store only the changed columns (`updated_at` ignored). `audit_strip()` removes `discord_id`, `discord_thread_id`, `email`, `evidence_paths`, `screenshot_paths` and `*_token`/`*_secret` keys; add new private columns there. `audit_log` is append-only for every role, including the owner and the service role, because test rows written to the live database can never be removed (tests must roll back). The site reads `audit_log_public` (actor name plus a `summary` like "Admin jake changed match ALP vs BRV (week 2): week 1 → 2"); the raw table is staff-only. The Discord hook `audit_log_notify_discord()` exists but isn't attached yet (bot step; needs Vault `discord_sync_url` and `discord_sync_secret`).
- Storage: `screenshots` (private) paths are `{matches|disputes}/{match_id}/{team_id}/<file>`; `team-logos` (public) paths are `{team_id}/<file>`.
- Tests: `supabase/tests/rules_test.sql` runs in one rolled-back block and must end with `ALL TESTS PASSED`. Run it (MCP `execute_sql` or the SQL editor) after changing rules or policies.
