# Heaven's Arena League Official Rulebook

These rules govern Season 1 of Heaven's Arena League (HAL), a free-entry, NA-only Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4 league. By registering, every player and captain agrees to them.

Effective: November 20, 2026

## 1. General

**1.1** HAL follows the current Call of Duty League (CDL) ruleset and Gentlemen's Agreements (GAs), except where this rulebook says otherwise. Where they conflict, this rulebook wins.

**1.2** All league times are set in Eastern Time (America/New_York). The website shows every time in your own time zone, with Eastern Time on hover.

**1.3** League staff interpret these rules. Admin decisions are final.

**1.4** Official league business happens on the HAL website and in the HAL Discord server. Agreements made anywhere else (private messages, other servers) do not count unless they are recorded on the site or with the bot.

**1.5** Entry is free. There are no fees at any point in the season.

## 2. Eligibility

> [!IMPORTANT]
> You must be 18 or older to play in HAL, and HAL is controller only on every platform. You confirm both when you set up your profile, and you can't withdraw either confirmation.

**2.1** Players must be 18 or older.

**2.2** HAL is open to players in North America only.

**2.3** Controller only, on every platform. PC players are allowed if they play with a controller.

**2.4** Admins may ask a PC player for a live screen-share during a match.

**2.5** You log in with Discord and must set up your player profile before joining or creating a team: your Activision ID, your platform, and the confirmations in 2.1 and 2.3.

**2.6** Your Activision ID must be your real, current ID. Each Activision ID can be registered to only one HAL account.

**2.7** Players who are banned may not play, captain or join a team until the ban ends.

## 3. Teams and Rosters

**3.1** A player may be on only one team per season.

**3.2** A roster has up to 4 starters and up to 3 subs, for a maximum of 7 players. The captain counts as one of the starters.

**3.3** Every team has one captain and may have one co-captain. The co-captain must be on the roster.

**3.4** Teams can only be created while season signups are open. The player who creates a team becomes its captain and first starter.

**3.5** New teams are listed as pending until an admin approves them. A team needs at least 4 players on its roster before it can be approved. Only approved teams are scheduled.

**3.6** Every player who logs in is listed as a free agent until they join a team. Captains add players by inviting them from the website. A player joins the roster only by accepting an invite. Invites expire after 7 days, and accepting one invite cancels all of that player's other invites for the season.

**3.7** Captains can only invite players while the team has an open roster spot.

**3.8** Captains may remove players, and players may leave a team. A captain must hand the captaincy to another rostered player before leaving.

**3.9** Each team has 3 roster changes per season. Once the regular season starts, every player added or dropped uses one change. Roster building before the regular season is free.

**3.10** Rosters lock about 2 weeks before the playoffs. League staff publish the exact lock date for each season. After the lock, no players can be added or dropped except by staff.

**3.11** A player who is dropped or leaves a team cannot join another team in the same division for 7 days.

**3.12** Team names, tags and logos must follow the conduct rules in Section 11. Staff may require a team to change any of them.

## 4. Substitutes

**4.1** Subs can be swapped in only between maps, never during one.

**4.2** Subs must be on the roster at least 24 hours before a match to play in it. This applies to every player: anyone who joins a roster less than 24 hours before a match is not eligible for that match.

**4.3** Emergency sub: if a team has fewer than 4 players available for a match (eligible under 4.2 and able to play), the captain marks which players are out and may request a free agent for that match only, on the match page. A team may use at most 2 emergency subs in a match, and no more than it is short. The free agent must accept the request, and then an admin must approve it. Players marked out cannot play in that match once an emergency sub is approved. The emergency sub does not join the roster, stays a free agent, and does not use a roster change.

**4.4** Emergency subs must be free agents (not on any team this season) and meet every rule in Section 2.

## 5. Season Format

**5.1** Up to 16 teams play in one division. If more than 16 teams register, the league splits into a Premier division and a Contender division, and staff place teams in each.

**5.2** A season has a 2-week preseason, an 8-week regular season with 2 matches per week, and a playoff for the top 8 teams lasting 2 weeks, followed by the offseason.

**5.3** Every match is a best-of-5 series. The higher seed picks first, and picks alternate after that.

**5.4** Standings are ranked by series wins.

**5.5** Ties in the standings are broken in this order:
1. Series wins
2. Map differential (maps won minus maps lost)
3. Head-to-head result between the tied teams
4. Round differential on deciding maps
5. Admin decision

**5.6** To play in the playoffs, a player must have played in at least 2 regular-season matches for their team.

## 6. Scheduling and Reschedules

**6.1** Default match nights are Tuesday and Thursday at 9:00 PM Eastern (8:00 PM Central). Staff may set different match nights or times for a season, and the website shows the schedule.

**6.2** A match can be rescheduled only if both captains agree on the website or with the bot, at least 24 hours before the default match time.

**6.3** The new time must fall in the same week as the original match: no later than Sunday at 11:59 PM Eastern of that week.

**6.4** If the captains do not agree in time, the default match time stands.

**6.5** Only one reschedule request per match can be open at a time. The team that made a request may cancel it until the other captain answers.

## 7. Match Day

**7.1** The bot posts match reminders in each team's private channel 24 hours and 1 hour before the match.

**7.2** Check-in opens 15 minutes before the match. The captain checks in exactly 4 players from the roster (or an approved emergency sub). Admins can review every check-in.

**7.3** A team that is 15 minutes late forfeits the first map. A team that is 30 minutes late forfeits the series.

**7.4** If a player disconnects in the first 30 seconds of a map, or before the first kill, the map is restarted. Otherwise play continues.

**7.5** Each team may call one tactical pause per series, of up to 5 minutes.

**7.6** The higher seed hosts the lobby unless both captains agree otherwise.

> [!NOTE]
> Lobby settings, modes, maps and Gentlemen's Agreements are covered in Section 8.

## 8. Game Settings, Maps and GAs

**8.1** HAL follows the current CDL ruleset and Gentlemen's Agreement (GA) list for Modern Warfare 4, as updated during the season.

**8.2** Mode order for a best-of-5: to be published by league staff.

**8.3** Map pool for each mode: to be published by league staff.

**8.4** HAL Gentlemen's Agreements: to be published by league staff.

> [!NOTE]
> League staff will publish the MW4 modes, map pool and GA list before the regular season. They are kept on the website and announced in Discord, and any change is recorded in the public audit log.

**8.5** Breaking a GA results in the penalty listed for it. Where none is listed, the offending team loses the map.

## 9. Reporting and Stats

**9.1** HAL uses double-entry reporting. After every match, both captains submit the full scoreboard on the website or with the bot: the series score, and for every map the mode, map, final score, winner, a scoreboard screenshot, and each player's kills, deaths and score for all 8 players.

**9.2** The series result is confirmed when both captains' series and map scores agree. Standings update when a result is confirmed.

**9.3** Player stats are compared field by field. Matching values are confirmed. Any value that differs becomes a mismatch in the admin queue, and an admin decides the correct value from the screenshots.

**9.4** If one captain has not submitted 24 hours after the match, the other captain's submission stands, as long as it includes screenshots.

**9.5** Submitting false results or edited screenshots is cheating and is penalised under Section 11.

**9.6** The site calculates K/D, plus/minus, average score per map, maps played, map and series win percentage, and team records by mode, from confirmed stats only.

## 10. Disputes

**10.1** Only a captain can open a dispute, and only for a match their team played.

**10.2** Disputes must be opened within 24 hours of the match, with evidence (screenshots, clips or recordings).

**10.3** Opening a dispute creates a private thread with both captains and the admins. Evidence is visible only to those people.

**10.4** Admins may request VODs or POV recordings from either team while deciding a dispute.

**10.5** Admin decisions on disputes are final.

## 11. Conduct and Integrity

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Cheating devices (including Cronus, XIM and strike packs) are banned. A team caught using one is banned for the season, and the player is banned from HAL permanently.

**11.1** Cheating devices and software: the team is banned for the season and the player is banned permanently.

**11.2** Exploits (unintended game behaviour that gives an unfair advantage): the offending team loses the map.

**11.3** Conduct violations (such as harassment, hate speech or threats): suspension or ban, at the admins' discretion.

**11.4** Admins may ask any team for VODs or recordings when investigating a report.

**11.5** Playing on someone else's account, or letting someone else play on yours, is cheating and is penalised under 11.1.

## 12. Penalties

**12.1** Penalties include warnings, map forfeits, series forfeits, suspensions, bans and removal from the league. Every penalty is recorded in the public audit log.

**12.2** A team that forfeits 2 series in a season is removed from the league, and its remaining matches are recorded as losses.

**12.3** A suspended or banned player may not play, captain or join a team until the suspension or ban ends.

## 13. Prizes

**13.1** The champion receives $200: $50 for each of the 4 starters.

**13.2** Prizes are paid by PayPal or Venmo within 7 days of the final.

**13.3** Only players who meet Section 2 and are on the champion's roster at the final are eligible for a prize share.

## 14. League Staff and Conflicts of Interest

**14.1** HAL staff roles are owner, admin and caster. Casters can only mark matches as broadcast and add their stream link.

**14.2** The league owner's team plays in HAL.

**14.3** Staff never take staff actions on a match or team involving a team they are rostered on. This covers results, stats, mismatches, disputes, check-ins, reschedules, roster decisions and penalties. The league database enforces it, so a rostered staff member cannot override it.

**14.4** Another admin handles anything involving a staff member's own team.

**14.5** Every staff action is recorded in the public audit log on the website, with who did it, what changed and when. Private information (emails, Discord IDs, dispute evidence) is never shown there.

## 15. Changes to These Rules

**15.1** League staff may change these rules during the season. Changes are announced in Discord.

**15.2** The full history of every change is public on GitHub, and the website shows the date of the latest change.

**15.3** A rule change does not apply to a match that was played before the change.
